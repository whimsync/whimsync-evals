The quarterly review meeting was held on the third floor of the Bangalore office. Attendance was strong, with forty two people joining in person and another eighteen dialing in remotely.

The primary agenda item was the migration of the billing pipeline from the legacy batch system to the new streaming architecture. Engineering estimated that the cutover would take roughly six weeks, assuming no major regressions surfaced during the shadow traffic phase.

Finance raised concerns about reconciliation during the transition window. It was agreed that both systems would run in parallel for at least two full billing cycles before the legacy system is decommissioned.
