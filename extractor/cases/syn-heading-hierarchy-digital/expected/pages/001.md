# Platform Operations Handbook

This handbook describes how the platform team runs production services.

## On Call Rotation

Each engineer serves one week of primary on call every six weeks.

### Escalation Policy

Pages that are not acknowledged within fifteen minutes escalate to the secondary.

### Handoff Checklist

Outgoing engineers must document all open incidents before the Monday handoff.

## Deployment Windows

Production deploys are allowed Monday through Thursday between ten and four.

### Freeze Periods

No deploys are permitted during the final week of each fiscal quarter.
