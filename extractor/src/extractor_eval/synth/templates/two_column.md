# Engineering Newsletter

The infrastructure team completed the move to the new data center last month. Latency for European customers dropped by nearly thirty percent after the migration.

The mobile team shipped offline support for the field inspection app. Inspectors can now complete forms without connectivity and sync when they return to coverage.

The security team rolled out hardware keys to every employee. Phishing related account takeovers have fallen to zero since the rollout began.

The data platform team released a self service dashboard builder. Analysts created over two hundred dashboards in the first week alone.

The support organization introduced a tiered routing model for inbound tickets. Billing questions now reach a specialist within an hour on average, compared with nearly a full day previously.

The search team rebuilt the ranking pipeline around learned embeddings. Click through rates on the first result improved by eleven percent during the controlled experiment.

The payments team completed certification for three additional regional card networks. Merchants in Southeast Asia can now accept local cards without a third party gateway.

The developer experience team cut median continuous integration time from nineteen minutes to seven. Most of the savings came from caching dependency installs and splitting the slowest test suites.

The reliability group published a new error budget policy for customer facing services. Teams that exhaust their budget must pause feature launches until stability metrics recover.

The design systems team shipped version four of the component library. Every product surface migrated within the quarter, removing over forty thousand lines of bespoke styling code.

The analytics team retired the nightly batch warehouse load in favor of change data capture. Dashboards now reflect production data within five minutes instead of the next morning.

The localization team added right to left layout support across the web application. Arabic and Hebrew speaking customers can now use the product in their native languages.

The procurement team renegotiated the primary cloud contract for a further three years. The new agreement includes committed use discounts worth roughly eighteen percent annually.

The internal tools team launched a single sign on portal for every back office application. Employees no longer need to manage separate passwords for expense, travel and payroll systems.
